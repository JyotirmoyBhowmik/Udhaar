## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, modern fintech environment tailored for personal and business credit ledgers (*udhaar khatas*). It balances mathematical rigor—anchored in SQLCipher encryption and cryptographic proof—with accessible, friendly financial interaction for everyday shopkeepers, merchants, and individuals.

The visual direction combines **Deep Royal Purple/Indigo Canvas Foundations** with vibrant **Emerald Green** (receivables, credit issued, positive cashflow) and **Coral Red** (payables, debts owed, urgent reminders). The aesthetic employs a hybrid of **Modern Corporate Fintech** and **Tactile Glassmorphism**:
- Soft, pill-shaped tactile interaction targets evoking physical stamps and verified receipts.
- Deep layered gradients that establish cryptographic depth and bank-grade privacy.
- Frosted glass containers and elevated cards with multi-layered diffused ambient shadows, paired with high-precision micro-typography for ledger math.
- High-contrast states that immediately distinguish whether the user is in a net-credit (positive/asset) or net-debt (liability) posture.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by a high-contrast triad designed for clear financial accounting:

- **Primary (`#4A154B`)**: A deep royal purple, transitioning to `#2E0854` and `#5F259F` across hero banners, top-level navigation, and master actions. It projects authority, security, and digital sovereign privacy.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` / `#00C853`)**: High-saturation emerald green. Used for positive financial statuses: **Receivables**, incoming balance, verified cryptographic receipts, and affirmative confirmations.
- **Tertiary (`#FF5252`)**: Vibrant coral red. Dedicated strictly to liability: **Payables**, money owed, overdue flags, and debt settlements.
- **Neutral (`#0B1220` / `#94A3B8`)**: High-contrast slate-navy for clear legibility on mathematical figures, currency markers (₹ and रू), and transaction timestamps.

### Semantic Status Pairing
- **Receivable Tint**: `#D1FAE5` surface with `#065F46` label and `#10B981` border.
- **Payable Tint**: `#FEE2E2` surface with `#991B1B` label and `#FF5252` border.
- **Verification & Cryptographic Badges**: Translucent amber `#FEF3C7` surfaces with `#F59E0B` icon accents and micro-borders (`#B45309`).

## Typography

The typography pairings articulate technical confidence and immediate legibility:

- **Display & Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Soft yet commanding geometric grotesque weights (700/800) give modern fintech prominence to marketing headings, dashboard net totals, and customer ledger summaries.
- **Body & Controls (`Inter`)**: Neutral, systematically neutral letterforms maintain crisp readability across balance statements, recipient phone numbers, and payment descriptions.
- **Verification, Tags & Ledger Data (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Applied to cryptographic hashes, SQLCipher status chips, VPA handles (`user@oksbi`), and transaction reference metadata to communicate cryptographic proof and ledger immutability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system is built around a structured **8-point mathematical scale** using a fluid grid system:

- **Mobile Viewport (< 640px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Primary action bars (e.g., "+ NEW UDHAAR") are affixed to a sticky bottom floating dock with safe-area spacing.
- **Tablet / Dashboard Viewport (640px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `2rem` margins. Split view for ledger contacts list on the left and active transaction stream on the right.
- **Desktop Marketing & Web App (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1280px` with `3rem` margins. Asymmetric hero arrangement juxtaposing heavy purple gradient waves with floating glassmorphic phone views.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Financial stat cards (Total Receivable vs. Total Payable) sit adjacent in a 2-column inline grid with a strict `0.75rem` gap to ensure parallel comparison without scroll fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered glassmorphism, tinted ambient drop shadows, and glowing accent borders:

1. **Flat Base Surface (`0B1220` or `#F8FAFC`)**: Ground level providing neutral contrast.
2. **Hero Wave Canvas**: Multi-stop radial and linear gradients spanning `#2E0854` (base) to `#5F259F` (apex) layered beneath floating 3D coins and cryptographic badges.
3. **Glassmorphic Security Shield & Containers**:
   - `background`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` in light mode, `rgba(22, 34, 56, 0.7)` in dark headers.
   - `backdrop-filter`: `blur(16px) saturate(180%)`.
   - `border`: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.
   - `box-shadow`: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(46, 8, 84, 0.18), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9) inset`.
4. **Receivable & Payable Summary Cards**:
   - Subtle tinted elevation. Green cards cast a soft emerald hue (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`), while Red cards cast a soft coral aura (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(255, 82, 82, 0.15)`).
5. **Floating Action Trigger**:
   - Primary floating action button uses deep directional shadow: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

The system relies on a **Rounded Level 2** foundation (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline) paired with selective pill geometry for interactive states:

- **Cards & Ledger Modules**: `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) to provide modern, approachable separation of entries.
- **Filter Chips & Status Badges**: Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical metadata from rectangular data panels.
- **Input Fields & Search Bars**: `rounded-md` (`0.75rem` / `12px`) with a soft inward inset.
- **Call-to-Action Buttons**: Extended pill styling (`1.5rem` / `24px` to `9999px`) on floating triggers, creating tactile touch surfaces for rapid thumb access.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Floating Action ("+ NEW UDHAAR")**: Vibrant Emerald (`#10B981`) or Purple (`#5F259F`) background, pure white bold text, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `14px 28px`, with a subtle vertical top gradient and an emerald glow shadow.
- **Secondary Actions ("Send Reminder", "Get QR/Receipt")**: Muted slate/navy surface (`#162238` or `#F1F5F9`), crisp iconography, `rounded-xl`, high-contrast text.
- **Verification CTA ("Inspect", "Verify Shared Link")**: High-saturation emerald teal button with instant hover scale (`1.02`) and high-contrast dark text.

### Stat Cards (Total Receivable vs. Total Payable)
- **Receivable Card**: Soft pastel mint container (`#D1FAE5`), uppercase micro-label ("TOTAL RECEIVABLE:"), bold headline currency stat in `#065F46`, and an embedded badge indicator (`RECEIVED` / `ACTIVE`).
- **Payable Card**: Soft pastel coral container (`#FEE2E2`), uppercase micro-label ("TOTAL PAYABLE:"), bold headline currency stat in `#991B1B`.

### Ledger Record Card
- Encapsulated white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px solid #E2E8F0` border.
- **Top Row**: Transaction direction badge (`GIVEN` in deep emerald dark pill, `TAKEN` in coral pill), payment mode badge (`UPI`, `CASH`, `BANK`), and right-aligned due date.
- **Identity Block**: Borrower/Lender name in 16px bold (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), masked contact number beneath, and large bold INR/NPR amount aligned right.
- **Details Footprint**: Principal amount, dynamic interest calculation (`8% / yr • Simple`), and remark note in italicized gray text.
- **Bottom Action Strip**: Inline dual buttons for "Get QR/Receipt" and "Send Reminder" (with instant WhatsApp integration styling).

### Filter Chips & Navigation Segmented Controls
- Segmented bar ("Active (1)", "People (2)", "History"): Pill containers with active state highlighted via white fill, dark navy text, and a crisp 1px outline.
- Ledger filter strip ("ALL", "Given", "Taken", "Overdue/Expired", "Settled"): Horizontal scrolling pill elements with subdued inactive backgrounds and high-contrast active fills.

### Cryptographic Proof & Security Badges
- Floating security shield: Golden amber gradient with 3D bevel effect, lock icon, and SQLCipher encryption tag.
- Monospace hash verification banner: `#FEF3C7` background with gold border, displaying two-party verification state and encryption key fingerprint.
