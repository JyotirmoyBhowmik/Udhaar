Complete Feature Specifications

Everything built into the Udhaar app

Detailed overview of all 12 functional modules in Udhaar—from instant dashboard filtering and partial repayments to party-wise PDF statements, two-party verification, and group bill splits.

1. Home Dashboard & Instant Filters

Core Ledger
  • Separate Active, People, and History tabs with live record counts.
  • Real-time global search by person name, note, amount, or phone digits.
  • One-tap filter chips for All, Owed, Borrowed, Overdue, and Settled.
  • Total Receivable and Total Payable summary cards with one-tap privacy eye masking (₹ • • • • •).

2. Compact & Inline-Expandable Cards

Quick Actions
  • Clean 2-row compact cards displaying party initials, name, Given/Taken pill, payment mode, and net remaining due.
  • Tap the chevron (⌄) to expand inline for masked phone number (98*** ***10), due date, and principal details.
  • Direct inline buttons for Transaction Chat, Get QR / Receipt, and Send Reminder.

3. Partial & Installment Repayments

Repayments
  • One-tap percentage chips (25%, 50%, 75%, 100% FULL) auto-calculate exact paise amounts.
  • Track payment mode per installment: Cash, Online, UPI, or Party Account.
  • Attach live camera photos (Proof cam) or gallery screenshots (Proof pic) to every repayment.
  • Toggle "Reported by other person (Requires my confirmation)" for incoming payment claims.

4. Structured Installment Schedule Planner

EMI Planner
  • Split any balance into custom installments with Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Monthly, or Custom intervals.
  • Choose None (₹0) or Fixed interest and preview every due date and installment amount live.
  • Final installment automatically absorbs any 1–2 paise division remainder (e.g., ₹1666.66 + ₹1666.66 + ₹1666.68 = ₹5,000.00).

5. People & Parties Khata Directory

Party Ledger
  • Selective contact import or .vcf vCard import—Udhaar never reads your phonebook without permission.
  • Sort parties by Name (A–Z), Highest Net Balance, Most Overdue, or Recently Active.
  • Automatic Repayment Trust Score Badge (e.g., ★ 95% GOOD) calculated from historical repayment punctuality.

6. Individual Party Statements & Exports

PDF & JSON
  • Complete per-person summary showing You will get (Lent), You will give (Taken), and net position per currency.
  • Export Visual Receipt / Statement (Image & PDF) or Formal PDF Statement with History of Changes.
  • Save or share machine-readable JSON statements and exchange UPI payment details directly.

7. Two-Party QR & Link Verification

Verification
  • Generate a scannable Verification QR Code for instant face-to-face confirmation or in-app scanning.
  • Every record generates a tamper-proof record fingerprint and a chronological History of changes.
  • Personal notes and attached proofs are locked with 256-bit end-to-end encryption so only the link holder can view them.

8. Granular Private Web Link Controls

Guest Portal
  • Set custom link expiry (7 Days, 30 Days, or 90 Days) and revoke shared links anytime.
  • Choose between WhatsApp OTP (Zero-Cost 6-digit code) or Private Link verification.
  • Toggle visibility of Note/Purpose, Attachments/Proofs, and My UPI QR, plus toggle guest actions (Confirm, Report a problem, I Have Paid, Propose Date).

9. Group & Split Payment Calculator

Bill Split
  • Split group dinners, rent, or trip expenses with Equal split, Custom amount, or By percentage.
  • Support both I paid (Others owe me) and Someone else paid (I owe them) with optional self-share inclusion.
  • Share the full split breakdown on WhatsApp and generate individual trackable Udhaar records in one tap.

10. Dual Currency & Paise-Exact Interest

INR & NPR
  • Native support for Indian Rupee (₹ INR) and Nepalese Rupee (रू NPR) with independent totals.
  • Optional Simple or Compound interest calculated per month or per year.
  • All accounting is stored in whole integer paise (₹1 = 100 paise) so floating-point errors never occur.

11. Encrypted Backup & Recovery Key

Data Safety
  • Backup your ledger to your own Google Drive or local storage locked with your personal backup password.
  • Generate an emergency Backup Recovery Key in case you ever forget your backup password.
  • Includes a built-in "Test my backup" button to verify backup decryption and integrity safely.

12. Polite Reminders in 5 Languages

Multi-Language
  • Full app interface and polite reminder templates in English, Hindi, Bengali, Assamese, and Nepali.
  • Schedule local due-date notifications and open pre-filled WhatsApp or SMS messages with one tap.
  • Optional biometric fingerprint / PIN app lock keeps your ledger private when handing your phone to others.
