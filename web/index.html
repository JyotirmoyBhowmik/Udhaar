Simple 3-Step Flow

How it works in 3 steps

Zero confusion. Both people stay on the exact same page.

1

Add the record

Enter the amount, due date, and the other person's name. It takes less than 10 seconds.

2

Send the link on WhatsApp

Udhaar prepares a polite message with a private confirmation link and 6-digit code for you to send.

3

The other person confirms

They open the link in any mobile browser, check the details, and confirm. They do not need to install the app.

Udhaar does not move or hold money

We are a record-keeping notebook, not a wallet or bank. All payments are completed through your UPI app or bank.

🤝

Udhaar is not a legal contract

Records and confirmation links provide mutual clarity between friends and family, but do not replace legal loan contracts.
