Privacy Policy & Data Deletion

Effective date: November 4, 2026 • Version 17.0

🛡️ In short: Your records stay with you

Udhaar is designed to keep your financial records locked on your own phone. We do not sell your personal data, track your location, or read your messages. Web confirmation links expire automatically and can be revoked or deleted at any time with a single tap.

1. On-Device Data & Hardware Encryption

Udhaar is an offline-first personal and party loan and ledger application. By default, 100% of your financial records, party profiles, and attachments remain strictly on your device inside an encrypted local database using AES-256 with Android Keystore.

2. Android Permissions & How They Are Used

  • Contacts (Optional): Requested only when you tap "Import from Contacts" to populate a person's name and phone number. Your address book is never uploaded in bulk to any server.
  • Camera (Optional): Requested only when you take a photo of a physical receipt or UPI payment slip to attach to a record.
  • Notifications (Optional): Used to deliver scheduled repayment reminders and web link confirmation alerts.

3. Optional Cloud Features (Google Drive Backup & Verification)

When you enable Google Drive backup, Udhaar stores only your client-side encrypted backup file inside your private Google Drive Application Data folder (appDataFolder). Backups are transferred directly between your Android device and Google's servers. Zero backup data or passwords are ever sent to the developer.

4. Guest Web Verification Portal (/u/ & Zero-Cost Code)

Udhaar provides a mobile-first web portal at https://udhaar.jyotirmoyb.com/u/ where the other person can view and verify transaction records without installing the app.

  • Cost-Free Authentication: Uses a 6-digit WhatsApp code sent directly between parties, avoiding third-party SMS gateway fees.
  • Cryptographic Fragment Secret: URL fragment anchors (#SECRET) are strictly processed by the client browser and never sent in HTTP request headers.
  • Rate Limiting: Enforces a maximum limit of 5 failed attempts per hour to prevent brute-force attacks.
  • Zero Trackers: The guest portal contains no advertising beacons, analytics trackers, or third-party cookies.
