Privacy & Data Safety

Privacy Policy & Data Deletion

Effective date: November 8, 2026 • Version 24.0 (Spark Mode)

🛡️ In short: Your records stay with you

Udhaar is designed to keep your financial records locked on your own phone. We do not sell your personal data, track your location, or read your messages. If you turn on sync, records are stored in Google's Firebase Firestore and can be read by that service and by you only through your account. Phone numbers are unverified contact details, never used for login or recovery. Web confirmation links expire automatically and can be revoked at any time.

1. On-Device Data & Hardware Encryption

Udhaar is an offline-first personal and party loan and ledger application. By default, 100% of your financial records, party profiles, and attachments remain strictly on your device inside an encrypted local database using AES-256 with Android Keystore.

2. Android Permissions & How They Are Used

  • Contacts (Optional): Requested only when you tap "Import from Contacts" to populate a person's name and phone number. Your address book is never uploaded in bulk to any server.
  • Camera (Optional): Requested only when you take a photo of a physical receipt or UPI payment slip to attach to a local record.
  • Notifications (Optional): Used to deliver scheduled repayment reminders and local sync notifications.

3. Optional Cloud Features (Google Drive Backup & Firebase Sync)

When you enable Google Drive backup, Udhaar stores only your client-side encrypted backup file inside your private Google Drive Application Data folder (appDataFolder). When you enable Firebase sync in Spark Mode, records are stored in Google Firestore with owner-only access rules and email-verification enforcement.

4. Guest Web Verification Portal (/u/ & Capability Links)

Udhaar provides a mobile-first web portal at https://udhaar.jyotirmoyb.com/u/ where the other person can view transaction details and submit proposals without installing the app.

  • Lender Approval Invariant: Guest actions (confirmations, dispute reasons, payment claims) are proposals only and never change the ledger until the lender accepts them in-app.
  • Cryptographic Fragment Secret: URL fragment anchors (#SECRET) are strictly processed by the client browser and never sent in HTTP request headers.
  • No Cloud Photo Uploads: Payment claims do not upload images to cloud buckets in Spark Mode.
  • Zero Trackers: The guest portal contains no advertising beacons, analytics trackers, or third-party cookies.

5. Zero Advertising & 100% Google Infrastructure

Udhaar operates entirely without advertising networks, third-party tracking beacons, or commercial data brokers. In Spark Mode, all cloud functionality runs strictly on Google Cloud / Firebase services (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Hosting, Remote Config, and Play Integrity). Your information is never sold, leased, or monetized.
