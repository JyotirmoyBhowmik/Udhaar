UDHAAR • PRIVACY & DATA SAFETY

Privacy Policy & Cloud Data Deletion

Application: Udhaar (com.jyotirmoyb.udhaar) • Effective & Updated: November 3, 2026 • Developer: Jyotirmoy Bhowmik

Udhaar is an offline-first personal and party loan/ledger application. By default, 100% of your financial records, party profiles, and attachments remain strictly on your device inside an encrypted SQLCipher database. Cloud features (Encrypted Google Drive Backup and Counterparty Mutual Verification) are strictly opt-in.

1. On-Device Data & Hardware Encryption

  • Encrypted Local Database: All ledger records, repayments, audit logs, and party details are stored locally in udhaar_ledger.db encrypted with 256-bit AES via SQLCipher and Android Keystore (MasterKey.KeyScheme.AES256_GCM).
  • Biometric & PIN Gate: Biometric authentication uses Android’s system BiometricPrompt API. Your fallback PIN is salted and hashed using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (60,000 iterations) and never leaves your device.

2. Android Permissions & How They Are Used

  • Contacts (android.permission.READ_CONTACTS — Optional): Requested only when you tap "Browse & Import Multiple Contacts" or "Import from Contacts" to quickly populate a counterparty’s name and phone number in your local People Directory. You may also use the single-contact system picker or import a .vcf file without granting this permission. Your address book is never bulk-uploaded to any server.
  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA — Optional): Requested only when you choose to take a photo of a physical receipt, invoice, or UPI proof to attach to a transaction.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Optional): Used to display scheduled local repayment reminders and real-time counterparty verification/chat alerts.

3. Optional Cloud Features (Google Drive Backup & Mutual Verification)

  • Encrypted Google Drive Backup (drive.appdata): When enabled, your ledger backup is encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM (derived from your passphrase via PBKDF2WithHmacSHA256 with 120,000 iterations) before being uploaded to your private Google Drive appDataFolder. Neither the developer nor third parties can access or decrypt your Google Drive backups.
  • Mutual Record Verification & Party Chat (Firebase Firestore & FCM): When you sign in with Google and explicitly share a verification link (https://udhaar.jyotirmoyb.com/u/...) or message a counterparty, the shared transaction snapshot, verification status, chat messages, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration token are stored in Google Cloud Firestore with TLS encryption in transit and strict participant-only security rules.

4. Zero-PII Telemetry & No Third-Party Ad Sharing

Udhaar contains no advertising SDKs and never sells or shares your personal or financial data with marketers. Internal error handling redacts phone numbers, UPI IDs, names, currency amounts, tokens, and PINs.

5. How to Delete Your Cloud Data & Account Records

Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can permanently delete all of your cloud-synced data directly inside the Udhaar Android app at any time:

  1. Open Udhaar and tap the Settings (Gear) icon in the top bar.
  2. Scroll to Cloud Verification & Account.
  3. Tap Delete My Cloud Data and confirm.

This immediately deletes your /user_profiles/{uid} profile, your authored /shared_records, your /chat_threads and messages in Cloud Firestore, revokes your device’s FCM push token, and signs you out—while keeping your local on-device ledger intact (which you can also clear at any time via Android System Settings → Apps → Udhaar → Clear Storage).

If you have uninstalled the app and wish to request deletion of cloud verification records associated with your Google account email or phone number, email admin@jyotirmoyb.com from that email address and your cloud records will be purged within 48 hours.
