Privacy & Data Safety

Privacy Policy & Regulatory Disclosures

Effective date: November 21, 2026 • Version 30.0 (Personal Ledger Architecture)

⚖️ Regulatory Disclosure: Personal Ledger Notebook, NOT a Lender

Udhaar does not lend money or process payments. Udhaar is strictly an offline-first personal record-keeping tool and digital ledger designed to help friends, families, and shopkeepers track money given and taken with mutual clarity. Udhaar does NOT provide personal loans, line of credit, instant credit, or peer-to-peer financing, and does NOT hold, intermediate, or transfer financial funds. Any UPI action merely opens the user's installed third-party UPI app using the lender's provided address.

🛡️ In short: Your records stay locked on your device

Udhaar is engineered with a strict privacy-first architecture. We do not sell your personal data, track your physical location, read your private SMS messages, or use commercial advertising networks. All transactions remain encrypted on your device. Optional cloud capability links expire automatically and require lender confirmation for every change.

1. On-Device Encryption & Data Storage

Udhaar stores your personal financial records locally using SQLCipher with AES-256 hardware-backed encryption via Android Keystore. The application does not transmit your ledger data to external servers unless you explicitly sign in and turn on Cloud Sync.

2. What Goes to Cloud Firestore (Spark Mode)

When you generate a shared web capability link for a transaction, a minimal summary is stored in Google Cloud Firestore under /guestLinks/{sha256(secret)} containing only the party names, amount in paise, currency, and due date. Directory listing is blocked by zero-trust security rules. Guest actions (confirmations, dispute reasons, date proposals, payment claims) are created as proposals with status PENDING and can only be applied to the ledger by the lender inside the Android app. Optional receipt images attached by guests are compressed as WebP documents under proof/image within strict size limits (< 680 KB) and deleted upon review.

3. Google Drive Backups

When you enable Google Drive backup, your complete database is encrypted client-side using PBKDF2 key derivation and AES-GCM encryption with your custom password before upload. Backups are stored in your private Google Drive Application Data folder (appDataFolder). The developer has no access to your Google Drive account, backup files, or decryption passwords.

4. Crashlytics & App Check

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to monitor application stability and Firebase App Check (Play Integrity on Android, reCAPTCHA v3 on Web) to prevent unauthorized API misuse. Crashlytics logs are scrubbed by an automated redaction engine: phone numbers, emails, names, monetary amounts, UPI IDs, and secret tokens are stripped before log dispatch. The user identifier is set to an anonymous installation ID, never a phone number or email address.

5. Android Permissions & Contacts Usage

  • Contacts (Optional): Requested only when you tap "Pick from contacts" to select a name and phone number. Your contact list is never uploaded, synced, or shared with any server.
  • Camera & Photo Picker (Optional): The app uses the privacy-safe Android Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia) without requiring broad storage permissions. Camera permission is used solely when capturing receipt photos on demand.
  • Notifications (Optional, Android 13+): Used to deliver scheduled repayment reminders and local synchronization updates.

6. Payments Policy & UPI Links

Udhaar does not process payments, handle credit or debit card data, or intermediate bank settlements. Payment buttons open the user's own UPI application on their device using a standard upi://pay link populated with the counterparty's provided UPI ID. Users must always verify the payee's name in their banking app before confirming any payment.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Open Settings → "Account & Web Access" → Tap "Delete my account and data". This immediately deletes your local database, encryption keys, Google Drive backup files, Cloud Firestore records, and Firebase Authentication user account.
  • Web Deletion: You can request complete deletion via our web portal at https://udhaar.jyotirmoyb.com/delete-account or by emailing our Data Protection Officer.
