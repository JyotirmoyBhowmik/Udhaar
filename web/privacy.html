UDHAAR • PRIVACY & DATA SAFETY

Privacy Policy & Cloud Data Deletion

Application: Udhaar (com.jyotirmoyb.udhaar) • Effective & Updated: November 4, 2026 (v16.0) • Developer: Jyotirmoy Bhowmik

Udhaar is an offline-first personal and party loan/ledger application. By default, 100% of your financial records, party profiles, and attachments remain strictly on your device inside an encrypted SQLCipher database. Cloud features (Encrypted Google Drive Backup, Counterparty Mutual Verification, and Guest Web Flow) are strictly opt-in.

1. On-Device Data & Hardware Encryption

  • Encrypted Local Database: All ledger records, repayments, audit logs, and party details are stored locally in udhaar_ledger.db encrypted with 256-bit AES via SQLCipher and Android Keystore (MasterKey.KeyScheme.AES256_GCM).
  • Biometric & PIN Gate: Biometric authentication uses Android’s system BiometricPrompt API. Your fallback PIN is salted and hashed using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (60,000 iterations) and never leaves your device.

2. Android Permissions & How They Are Used

  • Contacts (android.permission.READ_CONTACTS — Optional): Requested only when you tap "Browse & Import Multiple Contacts" or "Import from Contacts" to quickly populate a counterparty’s name and phone number in your local People Directory. You may also use the single-contact system picker or import a .vcf file without granting this permission. Your address book is never bulk-uploaded to any server.
  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA — Optional): Requested only when you choose to take a photo of a physical receipt, invoice, or UPI proof to attach to a transaction.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Optional): Used to display scheduled local repayment reminders and real-time counterparty verification/chat alerts.

3. Optional Cloud Features (Google Drive Backup & Mutual Verification)

  • Encrypted Google Drive Backup (drive.appdata): When you enable Google Drive backup, Udhaar stores only your client-side encrypted backup file inside your private Google Drive Application Data folder (appDataFolder).
    • Client-Side AES-256-GCM Encryption: Your backup is encrypted on your local Android device before upload using AES-256-GCM with a key derived from your secret passphrase via PBKDF2WithHmacSHA256 (120,000 iterations and random 128-bit salt).
    • Zero Data Sent to Developer: Backups are transferred directly between your Android device and Google's Drive servers. Absolutely zero backup data, metadata, or passphrases are ever sent to, accessed by, or stored by the app developer or any intermediary server.
    • Hidden App Data Isolation: Files in appDataFolder are strictly isolated to the Udhaar application and cannot be accessed by other Google Drive apps.
  • Mutual Record Verification & Party Chat (Firebase Firestore & FCM): When you sign in with Google and explicitly share a verification link or message a counterparty, the shared transaction snapshot, verification status, chat messages, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration token are stored in Google Cloud Firestore with TLS encryption in transit and strict participant-only security rules.

4. Guest Web Verification Portal (/u/ & Zero-Cost Verification)

Udhaar provides an accessible web portal at https://udhaar.jyotirmoyb.com/u/ where counterparties can view and verify transaction records in a mobile-first browser interface without needing to install the Udhaar app or create an account.

What Web Guests See

  • Transaction Core: Counterparty name, loan type (Given / Taken), principal amount in INR, transaction start date, and due date.
  • Installments: If the transaction is split into scheduled installments, guests see paid vs. remaining progress and due dates.
  • Private Note Controls: The creator’s private transaction note is hidden by default and is only displayed if the creator explicitly toggled "Allow guest to view note" when generating the link.
  • PII Masking: Sensitive identifiers are strictly masked on the web page: phone numbers appear as +91 98*** ***10 and UPI VPAs appear as ra*** ***ar@okaxis. The guest never has access to the creator’s full address book or other ledger records.

Zero-Cost WhatsApp Verification Code (No SMS Gateways)

  • Cost-Free Authentication: Udhaar does not use costly third-party SMS gateways, carrier text fees, or Meta WhatsApp Cloud API charges.
  • Cryptographic Fragment Secret: When a link is generated, the app produces a 24-byte random URL fragment secret (e.g., https://udhaar.jyotirmoyb.com/u/r/RECORD_ID#SECRET) and a 6-digit WhatsApp OTP.
  • Client-Side Anchor Isolation: URL fragment anchors (#SECRET) are never transmitted to cloud servers in HTTP request headers. The guest's browser extracts the secret locally.
  • SHA-256 Hash Storage: The Cloud Firestore database stores only the one-way SHA-256 hash of the secret and OTP. Plaintext verification tokens are never stored on any server.
  • Brute-Force Immunity: Constant-time hash comparison (crypto.timingSafeEqual) and automatic rate-limiting lockouts (maximum 5 failed OTP attempts per hour) prevent timing attacks and automated brute-force attempts.

Guest Actions & Immutable Audit Logging

  • Permitted Actions: Depending on permissions granted by the creator, guests can confirm terms, dispute with structured reasons, propose an alternative return date, submit a payment claim (with optional proof screenshot), or provide their repayment UPI ID.
  • State Machine Integrity: The record creator is strictly blocked by both Firestore Security Rules and Cloud Functions backend logic from confirming their own record. Only the designated counterparty can confirm.
  • Tamper-Proof Audit Trail: Every action appends an immutable entry to /shared_records/{id}/audit/{eventId} containing the event type, actor role ("guest" or "creator"), timestamp, and a cryptographic content hash snapshot. Neither party can alter past audit logs.

Zero Cookies & No Third-Party Trackers

  • The guest portal contains no tracking cookies, no advertising SDKs, no Google Analytics, and no marketing beacons.
  • Browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) is used exclusively on the guest's device to persist UI theme (dark/light), selected language (English, Hindi, Bengali, Assamese, Nepali), and the active OTP verification session.

5. Zero-PII Telemetry & No Third-Party Ad Sharing

Udhaar contains no advertising SDKs and never sells or shares your personal or financial data with marketers. Internal error handling redacts phone numbers, UPI IDs, names, currency amounts, tokens, and PINs.

6. How to Delete Your Cloud Data, Links & Backups

1. Delete Google Drive Backups

You can permanently delete all encrypted ledger backups stored in your Google Drive at any time:

  • In-App Deletion (Instant): Open Udhaar → Tap Settings (Gear) → Scroll to Backup & Sync → Tap Delete my Drive backups → Confirm Delete Permanently.
  • Via Google Drive Web Interface: Visit drive.google.com → Settings → Manage Apps → Udhaar → Delete hidden app data.
  • Revoke Google Access: Visit Google Account Permissions and remove Udhaar.
2. Revoke Web Links & Delete Guest Attachments

You maintain complete control over guest verification links:

  • Immediate Link Revocation: In the transaction verification card, tap Revoke Link. The link is instantly marked invalid and further access is blocked.
  • Revoke All Active Links: Open Settings → Web Verification Links → Revoke All Active Web Links. This invalidates every shared link across all your records.
  • Automated Expiration: Web links automatically expire after your chosen duration (7, 30, or 90 days), after which the portal is completely inaccessible.
  • Purge Guest Proof Images: Payment proof photos uploaded by guests to Cloud Storage can be purged directly by the creator tapping Delete guest data, or via automated daily retention cleanup.
3. Delete Counterparty Cloud Verification Records

To completely delete your cloud verification profile, shared link records, and mutual chat messages:

  1. Open Udhaar → Tap Settings (Gear).
  2. Scroll to Cloud Verification & Account.
  3. Tap Delete My Cloud Data and confirm.

This immediately purges your /user_profiles/{uid} profile, your authored /shared_records, your /chat_threads and messages in Cloud Firestore, revokes your device’s FCM push token, and signs you out.

If you have uninstalled the app and wish to request deletion of cloud verification records associated with your Google account email or phone number, email admin@jyotirmoyb.com from that email address and your cloud records will be purged within 48 hours.
