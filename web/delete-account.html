Account & Data Deletion Request

Effective date: November 21, 2026 • Full Data Control

⚖️ Regulatory Notice

Udhaar does not lend money or process payments. Udhaar is a personal record-keeping ledger tool. We do not store financial card numbers, bank credentials, or intermediate credit transactions. You maintain 100% ownership and control over your ledger data.

Option 1: Instant In-App Account & Data Deletion (Recommended)

You can immediately and completely delete your account and all associated records directly from within the Udhaar Android app without waiting for manual processing:

  1. Open the Udhaar app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (⚙️) in the top-right corner.
  3. Scroll down to Section 06: Account and web access.
  4. Tap the red button labeled "Delete my account and data".
  5. In the confirmation dialog, review the deletion scope and leave "Also delete encrypted backups from Google Drive" checked.
  6. Tap "Permanently Delete Everything".
What is permanently wiped during in-app deletion:
  • Your local encrypted SQLCipher ledger database (all transactions, contacts, repayment notes).
  • Hardware-backed AES-256 encryption keys in the Android Keystore.
  • All Cloud Firestore documents under /users/{your-uid}/**.
  • All capability guest links owned by you (/guestLinks/{linkId}) and their action proposals and receipt proofs.
  • Encrypted database backup archives stored in your Google Drive Application Data folder.
  • Your Firebase Authentication user identity.

Retention Policy Post-Deletion:

Upon confirmation of deletion, zero data is retained on our servers. Because Udhaar operates on Google Cloud Firebase with owner-scoped zero-trust security rules and does not sell or monetize information, your data is completely excised from cloud databases.
