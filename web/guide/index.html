Screen 1 of 12 — Onboarding
Chapter 01 • First Launch & Setup

Smart Records & Automatic Math

When you first open Udhaar, a 4-step walkthrough introduces how the app tracks money lent (Given) and borrowed (Taken) with zero rounding errors and complete device privacy.

Key Capabilities Introduced

  • Dual Currency Ledger (INR ₹ & NPR रू): Every transaction can be recorded in either Indian Rupee (₹) or Nepalese Rupee (रू). The dashboard and party statements maintain separate currency totals so INR and NPR balances are never accidentally mixed.
  • Precise Simple & Compound Interest: Configure optional monthly or annual interest rates. All calculations operate in integer paise (Long) with explicit HALF_UP rounding.
  • Smart Partial Payments: Every partial repayment or installment immediately updates the live net remaining balance and logs a permanent history entry.
  • No Mandatory Account or Phone Number: Tap Next → through the 4 onboarding cards to start using the offline notebook immediately without signing up.
Language & UPI Setup: You can switch the app language between English, Hindi, Bengali, Assamese, and Nepali or configure your personal UPI ID anytime in Settings (⚙️).
Screen 2 of 12 — Home Dashboard
Chapter 02 • Main Ledger View

Home Dashboard, Global Search & Filter Chips

The Home Dashboard gives you an instant overview of your entire lending and borrowing position, with one-tap filtering, privacy masking, and compact 2-row transaction cards.

Screen Elements & Controls

  • Top App Bar Icons: Quick-add party icon (+👤), Reminder Notifications bell (🔔), and App Settings gear (⚙️).
  • Primary View Tabs: Switch between Active (N) (open loans), People (N) (party-wise Khata directory), and History (settled/archived records).
  • Global Search Bar: Type any person's name, transaction note, phone digits, or amount to filter records in real time.
  • Status Filter Chips: Filter active records by All, Owed (money you gave and will receive back), Borrowed (money you took and need to pay), Overdue (past due date), or Settled.
  • Total Receivable & Total Payable Cards: The green card shows total money owed to you; the coral-red card shows total money you owe others. Tap the eye icon (👁️) on either card to mask amounts as ₹ • • • • • when in public.
  • Net Position Banner: Displays your net balance per currency (e.g., Net INR Position: +₹ 5,000).
  • Records Toolbar: Shows total matching count, a quick + Group Split button for shared bills, and the Newest sort menu (sort by Newest, Oldest, Highest Amount, or Due Soonest).
  • Compact 2-Row Transaction Card: Displays party avatar initials, name, direction pill (Given / Taken), payment mode badge (Cash / Online / UPI), net remaining amount, principal summary, and a Show details button.
Screen 3 of 12 — Inline Card Expansion
Chapter 03 • Fast Actions Without Leaving Home

Inline Expanded Transaction Card

Tap the chevron (⌄) on any dashboard transaction card to expand it inline for quick metadata inspection and one-tap actions, or tap Show details to open the full record sheet.

What Appears in the Expanded Card

  • Status & Metadata Chips: Direction badge (Given / Taken), payment channel (Cash), currency code (INR / NPR), due date status (No due date or exact due date), overflow menu (⋮), and collapse arrow (⌃).
  • Privacy-Masked Phone Number: Shows the party's contact number masked as 98*** ***10 alongside the original creation date and principal amount.
  • Tap for Repayment / Receipt Banner: Tapping the green callout opens the full repayment and receipt dialog immediately.
  • Bottom Quick-Action Bar:
    • Chat Icon (💬): Opens the dedicated in-app transaction chat and note thread.
    • Get QR / Receipt: Generates a shareable receipt slip or UPI payment QR code for the exact remaining balance.
    • Send Reminder: Opens the polite multi-language WhatsApp/SMS reminder composer.
Screen 4 of 12 — Record Detail & Repayments
Chapter 04 • Partial Payments & Settlement

Record Detail & Partial / Installment Repayment

Tapping Show details on a card opens the comprehensive Record Detail sheet where you can log partial repayments, attach payment proofs, generate receipts, or settle the loan in full.

Step-by-Step Repayment Controls

  • Net Remaining Due Header: Shows the live remaining balance in large numerals and a Show calculation details toggle that breaks down Principal + Accrued Interest − Partial Payments Paid.
  • Quick Percentage Chips: Tap 25%, 50%, 75%, or 100% FULL to auto-fill the exact paise-accurate repayment amount without manual typing.
  • Payment Mode Selector: Record how the installment was paid: Cash, Online, UPI, or Party Account.
  • Proof Cam & Proof Pic: Attach a live camera photo of a cash voucher or pick a UPI screenshot from your gallery as permanent proof for that specific installment.
  • Reported by Counterparty Toggle: Mark whether the payment was reported by the other person and requires your confirmation before reducing the balance.
  • Action Buttons:
    • + Add installment (CASH/UPI): Logs the partial payment and updates the remaining balance.
    • Split into installments: Opens the structured installment schedule planner (see Chapter 5).
    • Whatsapp & Receipt slip: Shares an updated text or image receipt showing the new balance.
    • Collect via my UPI QR: Displays a dynamic UPI QR pre-loaded with the remaining amount.
    • Mark as fully Paid back: Settles the entire record and moves it to the History tab.
Screen 5 of 12 — Installment Planner
Chapter 05 • Structured EMI & Schedule Builder

Split Into Installments Planner

Convert any lump-sum loan into a structured multi-part repayment schedule with automatic due dates and exact paise remainder absorption.

How to Configure an Installment Plan

  • Number of Installments & Start Date: Enter the total number of parts (e.g., 3) and select the first installment due date using the calendar picker.
  • Repayment Frequency: Choose Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Monthly, or Custom day intervals.
  • Interest / Extra Charge: Select None (₹0) for a zero-interest split, or Fixed interest to add an agreed flat fee across the schedule.
  • Live Installment Schedule Preview: Preview every installment's exact date and amount before saving. Notice how ₹5,000 split into 3 parts automatically calculates as:
    • #1 (06 Dec 2026): ₹ 1666.66
    • #2 (06 Jan 2027): ₹ 1666.66
    • #3 (06 Feb 2027): ₹ 1666.68 (final installment absorbs the 2-paise rounding remainder so the sum equals ₹ 5,000.00 to the exact paisa).
  • Save Installment Plan: Saves the schedule and enables automatic due-date tracking for each individual installment.
Screen 6 of 12 — Attachments & Audit Trail
Chapter 06 • Proofs & Change History

Attached Documents & Tamper-Proof Audit Trail

Scroll to the bottom of any Record Detail sheet to attach bills, receipts, or PDFs and inspect the record's verification fingerprint and edit history.

Features on This Screen

  • Attached Documents (Camera / Gallery / Doc/PDF): Attach multiple photos of handwritten slips, UPI screenshots, or PDF invoices directly to the transaction. All local attachments are encrypted on your device.
  • Verification Status Card: Shows whether the record is Not yet shared, Pending Verification, or Confirmed by Both Parties, with quick buttons for Send Web Link and QR / App Link.
  • Expandable Verification Details:
    • Record Fingerprint: Displays the unique tamper-proof digest calculated from the record ID, amount in paise, direction, and creation timestamp.
    • History of Changes: Lists every creation, edit, partial payment, and confirmation event with its exact date and time.
Screen 7 of 12 — Add / Edit Record Form
Chapter 07 • Creating & Updating Loans

Add or Edit Udhaar Record Screen

Tap the floating + New udhaar button on the dashboard (or Edit inside any record) to open the guided 3-step entry form with an expandable advanced section.

Form Fields & Options

  • Step 1 — Direction & Currency: Choose I Gave (Lent) or I Took (Borrowed) and select INR (₹) or NPR (रू).
  • Step 2 — Party & Amount: Pick an existing party from your Khata directory, import from contacts, or type a new name and phone number, then enter the principal amount.
  • Step 3 — When should it be paid back?: Quick-select In 1 week, In 1 month, Custom date, or Open-ended (no fixed expiry).
  • Collapsible "More details (Optional)" Section:
    • Payment mode: Select Cash, Online, UPI, or Other.
    • Charge or pay interest: Toggle on to configure Simple or Compound interest (% per month or % per year).
    • Scheduled reminder: Toggle automatic local reminder notifications before or on the due date.
    • Note or reason: Add context (e.g., "Shop inventory advance" or "Medical emergency").
    • Attachments & bills: Attach photos or PDF documents before saving.

Chapter 08 • Party-Wise Khata Ledger

People & Parties Directory

Switch to the People tab on the dashboard (or open the full People & Parties Directory) to view your relationship-level net balance with every person across all their transactions.

Directory Controls & Party Cards

  • Import & Add Party Buttons: Import contacts selectively via the system contact picker or .vcf vCard file, or tap + Add party to create a profile manually.
  • Multi-Field Search: Search across party names, phone numbers, UPI IDs, or custom relationship tags (e.g., Friend, Customer, Supplier).
  • 4 Sort Modes: Sort your directory by NAME (A-Z), HIGHEST NET BALANCE, MOST OVERDUE, or RECENTLY ACTIVE.
  • Repayment Trust Score Badge (★ 95% GOOD): Automatically computed from on-time vs overdue repayment history with that party.
  • Quick Party Card Actions: Pin favorites with the star icon (☆), view net receivable/payable badge (RECEIVE ₹5,000), open the party chat thread (💬 Chat), or tap + New loan to record a new transaction with that person pre-selected.

Chapter 09 • Statements, PDF Export & Direct UPI

Individual Party Khata & Statement Screen

Tap any person in the People directory to open their complete Party Khata page—featuring trust analytics, direct calling/WhatsApp actions, PDF/Image/JSON statement exports, and UPI settlement tools.

Everything Available Inside a Party Khata

  • UPI & Payment Details Exchange: Configure the party's UPI ID to pay them directly, or tap Share my payment details / Request their details.
Screen 10 of 12 — QR & Link Verification
Chapter 10 • Mutual Confirmation (In-Person or Remote)

Two-Party QR & Link Verification Sheet

Eliminate future misunderstandings by having the other person verify the record either face-to-face via QR scan or remotely via a verification link.

How Verification Works

  • Scannable Verification QR Code: When meeting in person, the other party can scan this QR code with their phone camera or inside their own Udhaar app to verify and import the exact record instantly.
  • Universal Web & App Link (udhaar.jyotirmoyb.com/u/...): Works in any mobile browser even if the other person does not use Udhaar.
  • Tamper-Proof Record Fingerprint: Displays the 32-byte digest (`fc27da28...`) that locks the amount, date, and direction so neither side can silently alter figures later.
  • End-to-End Encrypted Notes & Attachments: Personal notes and proof images are encrypted on your phone using AES-256-GCM before sharing; the decryption key stays in the URL fragment (`#k=...`) and is never sent to the server.
  • Sharing Channels: Tap Send verification on whatsapp, Copy link, or SMS / Other.
  • Paste Incoming Link: Received a link from someone else? Paste it into the "Have a verification link from the other party? Open it here" box at the bottom to verify and import it.
Screen 11 of 12 — Private Web Link Controls
Chapter 11 • Granular Guest Portal Permissions

Send Private Web Link Configuration

Before sending a browser confirmation link (`udhaar.jyotirmoyb.com/u/...`), you have full granular control over how long the link stays active, how the guest unlocks it, and which actions they are allowed to take.

Configurable Privacy & Guest Controls

  • Link Expiry Duration: Choose 7 Days, 30 Days, or 90 Days. Expired links automatically stop serving record data, and you can also revoke any active link with one tap.
  • Verification Method:
    • WhatsApp OTP (Zero-Cost): Generates a 6-digit verification code included in your WhatsApp message so only the intended recipient can unlock the link.
    • Private Link (Phone Last 4 Digits): Guests verify by entering the last 4 digits of their phone number.
  • Allowed Details Toggles: Choose whether to include Show Note / Purpose, Show Attachments / Proofs, and Show My UPI QR on Web Page.
  • Allowed Guest Actions Checkboxes: Enable or restrict specific guest actions on the web page:
    • Confirm — Guest agrees the amount and date are accurate.
    • Report a problem — Guest flags a discrepancy in amount or date with a reason.
    • I Have Paid — Guest submits a repayment claim with UTR/reference or screenshot proof.
    • Propose Date — Guest requests a new repayment due date.
Screen 12 of 12 — Group & Bill Split
Chapter 12 • Shared Trips, Rent & Group Expenses

Group & Split Payment Calculator

Tap + Group Split on the dashboard to split a restaurant bill, room rent, trip expense, or shared purchase across multiple people and automatically create individual Udhaar records for each member.

How to Split a Group Expense

  • Expense Details: Enter the Group or expense name (e.g., Dinner, Trip, Rent), total Amount (₹), and optional Due date for everyone.
  • Who Paid the Bill?: Select I paid (Others owe me) to create Receivable (`Given`) records for each member, or Someone else paid (I owe them) to record your payable share.
  • Include My Own Share Toggle: When enabled, divides the total bill among all members plus yourself so your own portion is deducted before generating the others' owed amounts.
  • 3 Split Modes:
    • Equal split: Divides the total equally in integer paise (any 1–2 paise remainder is handled cleanly without loss).
    • Custom amount: Enter exact rupee/paise amounts per person.
    • By percentage: Assign percentage shares totaling 100%.
  • Quick Member Selection & WhatsApp Summary: Tap existing contacts from your Khata pills or add members manually, share the full split breakdown via Share split on WhatsApp, and tap Save split records to generate individual trackable loans in one tap.

Appendix B • Accounting & Rounding Rules

Paise Precision, Interest & Installment Rounding Rules

How Udhaar guarantees that principal, interest, partial payments, and multi-part installments always add up to the exact paisa.

  • Whole-Paise Storage: Every rupee value entered by the user is immediately converted to integer paise (₹ 5,000.00 → 500000L paise). No Double or Float columns exist in the database.
  • Installment Division & Remainder Absorption: When splitting a total amount $T$ (in paise) into $N$ installments:
    • Base installment (parts $1$ to $N-1$): $\lfloor T / N \rfloor$ paise.
    • Final installment (part $N$): $T - \lfloor T / N \rfloor \times (N - 1)$ paise.
    • Example: Splitting ₹5,000 (500,000 paise) into 3 installments yields 166,666 paise (₹1,666.66), 166,666 paise (₹1,666.66), and 166,668 paise (₹1,666.68). Total = 500,000 paise (₹5,000.00).
  • Partial Repayment Allocation: Each confirmed partial payment reduces the net remaining obligation immediately and records its timestamp, payment mode (`CASH`, `ONLINE`, `UPI`, `PARTY_ACCOUNT`), optional note, and photo proof URI.
Appendix C • Data Safety & Recovery

Encrypted Google Drive Backup, Recovery Key & "Test My Backup"

Protect your ledger against phone loss or device upgrades without sacrificing privacy.

  • Client-Side Encryption Before Upload: When you back up to Google Drive or export a local backup file, Udhaar derives a 256-bit key on your phone using 600,000 iterations of PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 and encrypts the database with AES-256-GCM.
  • Backup Recovery Key: In Settings → Backup & Restore, you can generate and save a printable emergency Recovery Key so you can still unlock your backup if you forget your custom backup password.
  • "Test My Backup" Verification Button: Tap Test my backup in Settings anytime to run a non-destructive decryption and checksum dry-run in an isolated temporary sandbox—verifying your password and backup file integrity without overwriting your live records.
