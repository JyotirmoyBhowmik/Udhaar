Trust & Privacy Center

We believe you should know exactly where your records live, how they are protected, and what we never touch.

📱

1. Records stay locked on your phone

Your records are stored only in your phone's memory using SQLCipher encryption. We do not have a central database of your loans, transactions, or contacts.

Verified in code: UdhaarDatabase.kt
🔑

2. Backup password never leaves your phone

When you make a backup to your Google Drive, your phone locks it with your personal password using 600,000 rounds of PBKDF2 and AES-256-GCM. We cannot read or unlock your backup.

Verified in code: DriveBackupCrypto.kt
🌐

3. What a guest web link stores

When you share a confirmation link, our server temporarily holds only that single record and a salted phone number hash. It cannot see your other records or contacts.

Verified in code: guestWebFlow.js
⏱️

4. How long things are kept

WhatsApp verification codes expire in 15 minutes. Shared web links expire in 7 to 30 days. Uploaded receipt photos on the server are automatically purged after 90 days.

Verified in rules: storage.lifecycle.json
🗑️

5. How you delete your data

Deleting a record in the app removes it permanently. Deleting the app wipes all local records. To remove a shared web link instantly, tap "Revoke" in the app or use our deletion form.

Instant removal: /privacy.html#delete
💡

6. What Udhaar does NOT do

Udhaar does not move or hold your money, is not a bank, and does not provide legal loan agreements. All payments are done directly between parties using standard UPI apps or cash.
