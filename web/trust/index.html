Trust & Privacy Center

We believe you should know exactly where your records live, how they are protected, and what we never touch.

📱

1. Records stay on your phone by default

Udhaar operates offline-first using SQLCipher on your device. If you turn on sync, your records are stored in Google's Firebase Firestore and can be read by that service and by you only through your account.

Verified in code: UdhaarDatabase.kt & firestore.rules
🔑

2. Backup password never leaves your phone

When you make a backup to your Google Drive, your phone locks it with your personal password using 600,000 rounds of PBKDF2 and AES-256-GCM. We cannot read or unlock your backup.

Verified in code: DriveBackupCrypto.kt
📞

3. Phone numbers are unverified contact details

A free system cannot prove that a phone number belongs to someone. In Spark Mode, phone numbers are optional, saved with status "unverified", and labeled "Number not verified". They are used only to match contacts and in messages, never for login or account recovery.

Honest assurance: phoneStatus: "unverified"
🌐

4. Guest web links are capability proposals

When you share a confirmation link, the guest page receives a snapshot of that single record via a capability secret in the URL fragment. The guest can propose a confirmation or payment claim, but nothing changes your ledger until you tap Accept inside your app.

Lender approval required: guestLinks/{linkId}/actions
📷

5. Zero cloud photo uploads in Spark Mode

Cloud photo storage requires a paid billing account. In Spark Mode, payment claims store plain text reference notes (UTR or cheque number) with no photo uploads to cloud servers.

No billing-required Cloud Storage
🗑️

6. How you delete your data

Deleting a record in the app removes it permanently. Turning off sync or deleting your account removes your synced data from Firestore. Signing out of the web app wipes the encrypted browser cache.

Instant removal: /privacy.html#delete
