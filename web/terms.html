Terms of Service

Effective date: November 21, 2026 • Version 30.0 (Spark Mode)

🤝 In short: A personal record-keeping notebook, not a bank

Udhaar is a free, offline-first personal ledger notebook that helps friends, families, and small shopkeepers keep clear records of money lent and borrowed. Udhaar does not hold, move, lend, or collect money, and records created in Udhaar are for mutual clarity rather than formal legal loan contracts.

1. Nature of the Service (Not a Financial Institution)

Udhaar is purely a bookkeeping and record-keeping software application. We do not process payments, hold escrow, issue credit, charge interest on our own behalf, or act as a Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC), bank, payment aggregator, or debt collection agency in India, Nepal, or any other jurisdiction. When you use UPI QR codes or payment links generated by Udhaar, the payment happens directly between your own UPI / bank app and the recipient.

2. Offline Storage, Backups & Spark Mode Cloud Features

By default, your records stay encrypted on your phone using SQLCipher 256-bit encryption. Cloud features are optional and off by default. If you choose to sign in with Google or a verified email address and enable Cloud Sync in Spark Mode, your synced records are stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your account. You are responsible for keeping your device PIN, Google account, and Google Drive backup passphrase secure. If you lose your phone without an encrypted backup or active sync, we cannot recover your local-only data.

3. Guest Confirmation Links & Respectful Use

When you share a private web confirmation link (/u/), the recipient can submit up to a fixed number of proposals (confirming the record, reporting a dispute reason, claiming up to 5 payments, or proposing up to 3 return dates). Guest actions are proposals only and never alter your ledger unless you accept them in the app. You agree not to use Udhaar or WhatsApp reminder drafts to harass, threaten, spam, or defame any person. Phone numbers entered in Udhaar are unverified contact labels and are never used for login or account recovery.

4. Service Limits, Availability & Warranty Disclaimer

Udhaar v30.0 runs on the Firebase Spark (free) tier for optional cloud features, which enforces daily read/write quotas and does not include cloud photo storage. Udhaar is provided "AS IS" without warranties of uninterrupted cloud availability. Your local offline ledger continues working even if cloud quotas are reached. You may delete your local data or use the "Delete my cloud data" button in Settings at any time.
