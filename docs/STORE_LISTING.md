## 3. Full Description

**Udhaar is a private, offline-first personal ledger and digital khata notebook designed to help friends, families, roommates, and small shopkeepers keep clear, mutual records of money given and taken.**

Say goodbye to forgotten informal debts, confusing paper notebooks, and awkward payment reminders. With Udhaar, every entry is clearly accounted for, mathematically verified, and locked securely on your phone.

---

### 🛡️ 100% Private & Hardware Encrypted
- **Offline-First:** All your records stay strictly on your phone inside an encrypted SQLCipher database backed by AES-256 hardware keys via Android Keystore.
- **Zero Ads & Zero Tracking:** No third-party advertising SDKs, commercial data brokers, or analytics beacons. Your financial history is completely private.
- **Biometric Security:** Lock your ledger behind your device's fingerprint, face unlock, or a secure 4-digit PIN.

---

### 🤝 Mutual Clarity & Shared Verification Links
- **Two-Party Transparency:** Generate secure, capability-based web links for your transactions.
- **No App Installation Required for Friends:** Your friends can review the transaction summary, propose a due date extension, or submit a payment confirmation directly in their mobile browser.
- **Lender Approval Invariant:** Web proposals are never applied to your ledger until you review and confirm them in the app.

---

### ⚡ Smart Bookkeeping Features
- **Multi-Currency Support:** Record transactions in Indian Rupees (INR ₹) and Nepalese Rupees (NPR रू).
- **Partial Repayments:** Log installments with automatic balance recalculations down to the exact paise.
- **Installment Reminders:** Punctual, non-intrusive notification alerts for upcoming due dates.
- **Encrypted Google Drive Backups:** Back up your entire ledger directly to your private Google Drive application folder with zero-knowledge AES-256 encryption.

---

### ⚠️ IMPORTANT REGULATORY & COMPLIANCE NOTICE

> **UDHAAR DOES NOT LEND MONEY OR PROCESS PAYMENTS.**  
> - **Not a Lender:** Udhaar is strictly a personal record-keeping, ledger, and accounting utility. Udhaar is **NOT** a non-banking financial company (NBFC), bank, peer-to-peer lender, credit provider, or financial intermediary.  
> - **No Loans or Credit:** The app does **NOT** disburse personal loans, offer instant credit, facilitate borrowing, or provide microfinance.  
> - **No Payment Processing:** Udhaar does **NOT** hold, transfer, process, or transmit monetary funds. UPI payment buttons inside the app or guest portal simply open the user's installed third-party UPI application (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm) with the recipient's provided address directly between the two parties.
