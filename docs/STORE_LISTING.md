## 3. Full Description

**Udhaar is a private, offline-first personal ledger and digital khata notebook designed to help friends, families, roommates, and small shopkeepers keep clear, mutual records of money given and taken.**

Use Udhaar 100% locally on your phone with zero account required, or optionally verify your Google/Email account whenever you want two-party cloud verification, real-time in-app chat, and cross-device sync.

---

### Private, Offline-First & Hardware Encrypted
- **Start Locally or Verify Anytime:** Continue locally without cloud sync on first launch, or sign in with Google / verified email to enable optional cloud verification and messaging.
- **AES-256 Encrypted Local Vault:** All records stay locked on your device in a 256-bit SQLCipher database backed by Android Keystore keys.
- **Zero Ads & Zero Tracking:** No third-party advertising SDKs, no ad ID collection, and no commercial data brokers.
- **Biometric & PIN Lock:** Protect your khata with fingerprint, face unlock, or a PBKDF2-protected PIN.

---

### Mutual Clarity, Two-Party Chat & Safety Controls
- **Verified Two-Party Records:** Share clear transaction summaries and verification links with counterparties so both sides agree on exact amounts and dates.
- **In-App Thread & Direct Chat with Safety Controls:** Clarify repayments or request due-date extensions in-app, with built-in **Mute**, **Block**, and **Report** controls for both threads and users — enforced server-side so blocked users cannot send messages.
- **Lender Approval Invariant:** Counterparty web proposals are never applied to your khata until you review and accept them in the app.

---

### Built for Speed, Accessibility & 5 Regional Languages
- **Multi-Currency Exact Paise Engine:** Track Indian Rupees (INR ₹) and Nepalese Rupees (NPR रू) down to the exact paise with simple/compound interest, partial repayments, installment plans, and group splits.
- **5 Complete Languages:** Full native translations in English, Hindi, Bengali, Assamese, and Nepali.
- **Accessible & Fast:** Smooth Paging 3 history and search, `>= 48 dp` touch targets, TalkBack semantics, and tested at `200%` system font scale.
- **Encrypted Google Drive Backups:** Optional zero-knowledge AES-256-GCM backups to your private Google Drive appDataFolder.

---

### IMPORTANT REGULATORY & COMPLIANCE NOTICE

> **UDHAAR DOES NOT LEND MONEY OR PROCESS PAYMENTS.**  
> - **Not a Lender:** Udhaar is strictly a personal record-keeping, ledger, and accounting utility. Udhaar is **NOT** a bank, NBFC, peer-to-peer lender, or credit provider.  
> - **No Loans or Credit:** The app does **NOT** disburse loans, offer credit, or mediate borrowing.  
> - **No Payment Processing:** Udhaar does **NOT** hold or transmit money. UPI buttons simply open your installed third-party UPI app directly between the two parties.
